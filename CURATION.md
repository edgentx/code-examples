# Curation policy

**May be published**: fresh, minimal reimplementations of engineering patterns; house standards
excerpts written for this repo; synthetic or public sample data; VForce agent product output in
the labeled product-demonstration section only.

**Never published**: platform source code, anything copied verbatim from private repositories,
tenant or customer data, credentials, internal hostnames or endpoints, schema dumps from live
systems.

**Attribution rule**: work produced by the VForce agent platform is always labeled as product
output and never presented as hand-authored team engineering. Team-capability citations in
proposals draw only from the team-built section.

**Process**: additions arrive by pull request; the secrets scan must pass; a reviewer confirms
the section placement and the checklist above before merge.
